# Selfsame Credential Transfer

Selfsame credential transfer sends one [[SPEC-004-application-scoped-identity|application-account device grant]]
through an approved, intent-bound pairing ceremony.

The pairing layer transports opaque credential bytes. The Selfsame acceptance predicate remains
the only authority that turns those bytes into an accepted device grant.
