# UX Heuristics

This page defines the interface heuristics referenced by Selfsame screen specifications.

## Jakob's Law

Interfaces follow established platform conventions unless a recorded decision justifies a deviation.

## Doherty Threshold

Interfaces provide feedback quickly enough to preserve a person's sense of direct manipulation.

## Fitts's Law

Important targets are large and close enough for reliable pointing and touch input.

## Hick's Law

Interfaces bound visible choices and disclose secondary choices progressively.

## Miller's Law

Interfaces group information into small, meaningful chunks instead of one undifferentiated list.

## Von Restorff Effect

The primary action remains visually distinct from nearby alternatives.

## Goal-Gradient Effect

Progress indicators make the remaining distance to a goal visible.

## Peak-End Rule

The most intense moment and terminal result receive deliberate design attention.

## Aesthetic-Usability Effect

A polished surface never substitutes for task completion, refusal, and accessibility evidence.
